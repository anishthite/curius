Curius Links

About this list

A quiet, public view of the links Curius readers save, return to, and mark up.

What appears here

Each row is one saved link from the public Curius crawl. Its title opens the original source. The line beneath it shows the activity Curius has seen around that link and when it was last active.

  • Popularorders links by the activity score below.
  • Risinggives recent activity more weight, so newly active links can surface.
  • Newestorders them by their most recent save or highlight activity.
  • Pointsgive a compact signal of attention, not a judgment of quality.

How links are ranked

Popularity favors activity shared across people, while still crediting the total number of highlights.

Link score

Slink = 3usave + 5umark + h

usave is the number of distinct savers, umark is the number of distinct readers who highlighted the link, and h is the total number of highlights.

Rising score

Srising = Slink × 2−d / 14

d is days since the link’s latest visible save or highlight activity. The attention score halves every 14 days, so this is a time-weighted activity view rather than a measurement of change between crawls.

A few terms

  • Savera distinct Curius reader who saved the link.
  • Reader marked ita distinct reader who made at least one highlight on the link.
  • Highlighta saved passage associated with the link.

Scope

This is a generated snapshot of public Curius activity. Counts can change as the crawl refreshes, and a link’s position is only a reflection of the activity visible in that snapshot.
